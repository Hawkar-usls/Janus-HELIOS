# HELIOS — Market & License Position

**Review date:** 2026-08-31  
**Purpose:** buyer/licensor positioning, not a legal opinion, patentability opinion, valuation, or freedom-to-operate opinion.

## Executive finding

The market review found strong predecessors for **individual HELIOS ingredients**, but did not identify a public commercial product exposing the same complete maintained architecture as one reusable B2B control plane.

The safe claim is therefore:

> **As of the 2026-08-31 market review, we found predecessors for individual components, but did not identify a public commercial product exposing the same complete maintained HELIOS architecture as one licensable B2B control plane.**

This is deliberately narrower than “nobody has ever combined games and compute.” That broader claim would be false.

## Licensing economics — market sanity check

The proposed HELIOS commercial structure is consistent with common technology/OEM licensing patterns:

- WIPO describes licensing compensation as a lump sum, royalties, other benefits, or a combination; it also recognizes field, territory, duration and rights limitations.
- NASA publicly describes non-exclusive patent licenses with typical upfront fees around **$5k–$10k** and negotiated running royalties generally around **3%–7%**, with lower terms possible for less mature technology/startups.
- WIPO describes running royalties and minimum royalty payments as standard licensing mechanisms.
- Public OEM-software licensing references commonly describe revenue-share / royalty structures in low single-digit to low double-digit ranges depending on contribution and volume.

Sources:
- https://www.wipo.int/en/web/ip-business-moments/earn
- https://technology.nasa.gov/license
- https://www.wipo.int/fr/web/ip-commercialization/w/blog/how-to-set-intellectual-property-royalty-rates-in-a-licensing-agreement

### HELIOS indicative opening structure

**This is a negotiable opening framework, not a valuation, binding offer, market quote or guaranteed price.**

```text
PILOT LICENSE / INTEGRATION RIGHT
indicative discussion range: USD 10k–25k

THEN, IF COMMERCIALIZED
2%–5% of HELIOS-attributable compute revenue
OR
an agreed fixed amount per monetized/verified device-hour

OPTIONAL
annual minimum only when meaningful exclusivity is granted
```

The exact denominator matters. “HELIOS-attributable compute revenue” must be contractually defined to avoid accidentally taxing unrelated casino GGR, unrelated cloud revenue, taxes, pass-through energy, third-party provider charges or support services.

## Science / Public Benefit Discount

HELIOS should support a separately approved public-benefit policy:

```text
QUALIFIED SCIENCE / PUBLIC BENEFIT WORKLOAD
               ↓
HELIOS PLATFORM ROYALTY
REDUCED OR ZERO BY WRITTEN APPROVAL
```

This does **not** imply that electricity, provider, hosting, security, regulatory, settlement or third-party costs disappear. It only concerns the HELIOS licensor's own commercial royalty.

Candidate qualifying classes:
- bona-fide academic/nonprofit scientific research;
- medical/public-health research;
- humanitarian/public-interest computation;
- independently reviewed open scientific workloads.

Excluded by default:
- disguised commercial workloads;
- gambling economics presented as charity;
- cryptocurrency mining merely labelled public benefit;
- workloads without a legitimate owner/verifier or required legal/security review.

## IP boundary

Low entry price must not equal weak IP protection.

Preferred principles:
- HELIOS Core remains licensor Background IP;
- licensee may integrate and improve HELIOS inside the licensed field;
- no resale of HELIOS source as a standalone product;
- no use of disclosed protected implementation/materials to create a substitute clone for the purpose of avoiding agreed royalties;
- field-of-use, territory, duration and sublicensing are explicit;
- exclusivity requires milestones and/or minimum annual consideration;
- shelving the technology causes exclusivity to lapse;
- generic/core improvements should have a negotiated license-back or shared-use mechanism, while licensee-specific proprietary adapters/backends may remain licensee property;
- JANUS I0 remains separately scoped Background IP unless expressly licensed.

Actual enforceability and wording require counsel in the governing jurisdiction.
