# JANUS HELIOS — Excluded Assets Schedule

> Transaction-preparation document. Final wording must be reviewed for the transaction jurisdiction.

Unless expressly added to the Purchased Assets in a signed definitive agreement, a HELIOS-only acquisition **excludes**:

## JANUS ecosystem assets

- `Hawkar-usls/DIVINE_REALM`;
- `Hawkar-usls/SSlot`;
- `Hawkar-usls/janus-distributed-ai-swarm`;
- `Hawkar-usls/janus-meta-registry`;
- `Hawkar-usls/janus-io` / `JANUS I0`, including its private runner snapshots, evidence corpus, structured-traversal scheduler implementations, research controls, experimental artifacts and non-HELIOS research know-how;
- all other JANUS, DemiHead, COSMOS, SCOBY, research, personal or unrelated repositories and artifacts;
- source code, datasets, prompts, journals, experiments and know-how not embodied in the agreed HELIOS closing snapshot.

## Background IP and general know-how

- seller's general programming, architecture, distributed-systems and product know-how;
- generalized concepts that pre-date HELIOS or were independently developed outside the Purchased Assets;
- background implementations retained by the seller, subject to any license expressly granted to the buyer;
- the full `JANUS I0` scheduler, traversal methodology implementations and private evidence corpus except to the extent an express written licence or assignment specifically grants them;
- HELIOS bridge contracts or interoperability surfaces do not by themselves transfer ownership of the background repository or implementation they interface with;
- methods that are public-domain, open-source or otherwise available to third parties under their original terms.

## Future work

- future inventions not specifically commissioned under a post-closing statement of work;
- future JANUS projects;
- future improvements created independently after closing without use of buyer confidential information, unless a signed agreement says otherwise.

## Personal and operational accounts

- seller's personal GitHub account;
- seller's personal email accounts;
- personal cloud/storage accounts;
- passwords, authentication factors and recovery credentials;
- unrelated domains, social accounts and analytics properties.

A buyer should receive transferred repositories/assets or buyer-owned replacement accounts — never seller personal account credentials.

## Existing third-party/open-source rights

No transaction purports to assign ownership of third-party or open-source material beyond the rights available under its original license. Required notices and licenses survive the transfer.

The optional NerdMinerV2 compatibility path does not transfer ownership of `BitMaker-hub/NerdMiner_v2`; that project remains third-party MIT-licensed software under its original terms.

## Provider and partner relationships

Past outreach, conversations or introductions do not constitute transferable contracts, partnerships, exclusivity or guaranteed access unless a counterparty has expressly consented and the definitive agreement lists the contract as an assigned contract.

## Regulatory approvals

No gaming licence, regulator approval, RNG/RTP certificate, cryptocurrency/financial licence, data-protection certification or data-center certification is included unless an exact transferable approval is expressly listed.
