# JANUS HELIOS — Brand & Marks Schedule

> Transaction-preparation schedule. It does not establish trademark registration or clearance.

## Separate asset class

Source-code ownership and brand/trademark rights are separate. A buyer does not receive a name or mark merely because source code containing that name is transferred.

Potential names referenced by the project include:

- JANUS HELIOS
- HELIOS
- PLAYGRID
- DIVINE_REALM
- SSlot
- GRIDJACK

## Current representation boundary

This repository does **not** represent that every listed name is a registered trademark, registrable in every jurisdiction, or free of third-party conflicts.

Before a definitive acquisition:

- conduct clearance in the intended territories/classes if the buyer wants a brand;
- identify any actual registrations/applications by exact number and owner;
- confirm assignability;
- list transferred marks expressly in the Purchased Assets Schedule or a definitive agreement exhibit.

## Default HELIOS-only transaction

Unless the definitive agreement says otherwise:

- source/code transfer does not imply transfer of unrelated JANUS ecosystem branding;
- `DIVINE_REALM`, `SSlot` and other child/product names remain excluded with their repositories;
- seller retains all names/marks not expressly assigned;
- buyer may rebrand the acquired product if its definitive agreement grants the necessary product rights.

## Closing exhibit fields

For each mark actually transferred, record:

```text
mark/name
word/device mark
jurisdiction
class(es)
registration/application number (if any)
registered owner
status
assigned? yes/no
associated domain/social assets

```
